## How to use a second monitor

With two or more monitors, use Multi-Monitor Mode. The map stays on your first monitor. The Dispatcher and the calls you open in windows go into one large window, the workspace, on another monitor. To turn it on, see [Pick your display mode](30-your-settings.md#pick-your-display-mode).

### Open the workspace

1. Press **Workspace**, at the top right of the Dispatcher. A new window opens.
2. Drag the new window onto your other monitor, and maximize it.

The Dispatcher is now on the right side of the workspace. Calls you open in windows go to its left (see [Open a call in its own window](83-several-calls.md#open-a-call-in-its-own-window)). If the workspace is closed, the window button opens it.

- **Show on Map** moves the map on your first monitor.
- In CloudTAK’s menu, the Dispatcher now says it is open in its workspace window. **Show workspace** brings the workspace to the front, if the browser allows it. **Dock back to panel** closes the workspace and puts the Dispatcher back in the menu.

> [!TIP]
> **No new window?** Your browser blocked it, and a yellow line in the list says so. Until you allow it, the Dispatcher and the calls open over the map instead. Allow pop-ups for this site (look for a blocked pop-up icon or bar near the address bar). Then press **Dock back to panel** in the menu if it is there, and press **Workspace** again.

### Arrange the calls in the workspace

The calls share the space to the left of the Dispatcher by themselves:

- One call fills the space. Two split it in half, three in thirds, and so on.
- When there is no room left for another call at full size, the next one shares a spot with a call already there. The calls in that spot show as tabs along its top. Press a tab to bring that call to the front.
- On a monitor turned on its side, the calls stack from the top down.
- Close a window (**✕** at its top right) and the others take back its room.

To put a call somewhere else, drag its title bar. To change its size, drag its bottom right corner. It then stays where you put it, above the others. To put it back in line with them, press **Line up** on its title bar.

### Find the calls with no unit

In Multi-Monitor Mode, the list has three tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Unassigned** | Open calls with no unit working them: none sent yet, or every unit has cleared. Send a unit, or close the call. |
| **Active** | Open calls with at least one unit assigned or on scene. |
| **Closed** | Closed calls. |

A new call starts under **Unassigned**. When you send a unit, it moves to **Active**. If every unit on it clears, it goes back to **Unassigned**.

The cards in the list have no labels in this mode, because the tab already says it. The yellow stripe still marks a call that is open in its own window.

### If the workspace closes

The workspace closes when you press **Dock back to panel**, when you close the workspace window, or when the CloudTAK tab on your first monitor is closed or reloaded. The Dispatcher goes back to CloudTAK’s menu, and the calls stay on the board. Press **Workspace** there, and the workspace opens with the same calls in it. Anything you typed in those windows but didn’t save is gone.
