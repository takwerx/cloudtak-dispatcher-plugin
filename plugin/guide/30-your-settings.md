## Your own settings

The line under the Dispatcher’s title shows your CloudTAK callsign. At the right of that line is **TAK-CAD**. It is for agencies with a TAK-CAD server: leave it alone.

### Sign in with your employee number

> [!NOTE]
> **Employee numbers.** Your employee number is how the incidents you create, the notes you add and the changes you make are recorded as yours. They also show on the report. If a yellow bar says **Sign in with your employee number**, type your number and press **Sign in**. Your number then shows after your callsign, with **Sign out** at the right of that line. At shift change, press **Sign out** so the next person can sign in and their work is recorded under their own number. The bar shows once your event is open. If you don’t see it, your agency doesn’t use employee numbers.

### Pick your display mode

With two or more monitors, use Multi-Monitor Mode (see [How to use a second monitor](86-second-monitor.md)).

1. Press **Settings**, at the top right of the Dispatcher.
2. Under **Display Mode**, pick **Multi-Monitor Mode**. **Saved on this computer** shows for a moment.
3. Press **← Back**, if Dispatcher Settings is still showing.

This changes only this computer. Other desks keep their own setting, even if they use the same CloudTAK login. To go back, pick **Single Monitor Mode (default)**. If you do that in the workspace, the workspace closes and the Dispatcher goes back to CloudTAK’s menu.

Changing the mode closes the call windows that are open. In Multi-Monitor Mode they come back when you press **Workspace**; in Single Monitor Mode, when you go back to the list.
