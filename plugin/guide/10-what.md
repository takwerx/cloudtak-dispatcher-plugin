## What the Dispatcher is for

The Dispatcher is a simple dispatch board inside CloudTAK. It keeps track of calls for service and the units on each call. Every open call shows on the map.

Use it:

- for planned events, such as a fair or a festival;
- as a backup when your main CAD is down.

Everyone working the event sees the same board. A change made on one screen shows on the others within about 15 seconds. Field units see the calls on their TAK map.

To open this guide, press **User Guide** at the bottom of the **Events** list. If an event is open, press **Switch** first, under the event’s name, to get to that list. The guide opens in its own panel over the map, so you can keep it open while you work. In Multi-Monitor Mode, it opens on the monitor with the map.

- To get back to the list of topics, press **Contents** at the top of the guide.
- To move the guide, drag the dotted handle at its top left.
- To close it, press **✕** at its top right.

### What an Event is and what an Incident is

- **Event**: the container for one situation, such as a county fair, a storm or a CAD outage. You open one event and work in it until the situation is over.
- **Incident**: one call for service inside an event. Each incident gets its own number, such as FAIR-001.

For example, the event **COUNTY FAIR** holds the incidents FAIR-001 (Medical Emergency), FAIR-002 (Missing Person) and FAIR-003 (Fight).

> [!NOTE]
> **A note on these words.** Your CAD or records system may use them differently. Some agencies call each call an “event” and use “incident” for reports. In this tool, an Incident is one call. It is not a report number and not a NIBRS incident. If a call needs a report, add the report number to the incident.
