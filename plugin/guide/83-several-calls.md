## How to work several calls at once

The Dispatcher panel shows one incident at a time. To keep several calls in front of you, open each one in its own window.

### Open a call in its own window

1. In the list, press the incident’s card to open it.
2. Press the window button at the top right of the incident, on the same line as **← Back**. When you point at it, it says **Undock**.

The incident opens in its own window, and the panel goes back to the list. Do the same for the next call.

- The window is the whole incident. Work the call there the same way: units, notes, callers, close and reopen.
- Anything you were typing in the panel goes with it, except a location change you had not saved.
- In Single Monitor Mode, the usual setting, the window floats over the map: the first one at its top left, the next ones to its right. If you popped the Dispatcher out, the windows open beside it. In Multi-Monitor Mode, it opens in the workspace on your other monitor (see [How to use a second monitor](86-second-monitor.md)).
- The list shows how many calls are in windows, such as **2 undocked**. The card of each of those calls has a yellow stripe on its left edge. In Single Monitor Mode it also says **Undocked**.
- For a call that already has its window, the window button is greyed out.

### Move or close a window

- To move a window, drag the dotted handle at its top left.
- To change its size, drag its bottom right corner.
- To close a window, press **✕** at its top right. The call stays on the board. To open it in the panel again, press its card in the list.
- To close every window, press **Close all**, on the **undocked** line above the list.

Windows close when you switch events, and come back when you open that event again. After you reload CloudTAK, they come back when the Dispatcher opens (in Multi-Monitor Mode, when you press **Workspace**).

In the workspace, windows move a little differently (see [Arrange the calls in the workspace](86-second-monitor.md#arrange-the-calls-in-the-workspace)).
