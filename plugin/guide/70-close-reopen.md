## How to close and reopen an incident

Close each incident when the call is over.

### Close an incident

1. Open the incident: press its card in the list, or go to its window.
2. Press **Close Incident** at the bottom.
3. Pick how the call ended: **Cleared - No Report**, **Report**, **Unfounded**, **Warning**, **Gone on Arrival** or **Canceled**. For anything else, press **Other** and type a few words in the box beside it.
4. Press **Close Incident** again. It turns yellow once you have picked (with **Other**, once you have typed).

The Dispatcher goes back to the list you came from, and the incident moves to the **Closed** tab. Any unit still on it is cleared at the close time. The call comes off field units’ maps. With **Broadcast to channel**, field units first see it marked CLOSED for a while (60 minutes, unless your admin changed it).

If the incident is in its own window, the window stays open and shows it as closed. Press **✕** to close the window.

Changed your mind before step 4? Press **Cancel**.

### Reopen an incident

1. Press the **Closed** tab.
2. Press the incident’s card.
3. Press **Reopen** at the bottom.

The incident goes back to **Active** (in Multi-Monitor Mode, to **Unassigned**, since its units were cleared) and back on field units’ maps. Its disposition is cleared, so you pick one again when you close it. The notes still show the first close.

With employee numbers, **Reopen** asks why. Type the reason and press **Reopen Incident**.
