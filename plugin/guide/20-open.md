## How to open the Dispatcher

1. In CloudTAK, press the menu button (☰) at the top right.
2. Press **Dispatcher** (headset icon). If you don’t see it, type `Dispatcher` in the **Search...** box.

The Dispatcher opens at the side of the map. It may say **Connecting…** for a few seconds, then it shows the **Events** list: **+ New Event** at the top right, one line for each event, and **User Guide** at the bottom. If you reload the page while the Dispatcher is open, it opens again by itself.

At the top right of the Dispatcher are two buttons (once it is popped out, only **Settings**):

- **Settings** opens **Dispatcher Settings**. Leave those to your admin (see [How to set up the Dispatcher (for admins)](95-admin-setup.md)), except **Display Mode**, the first one (see [Pick your display mode](30-your-settings.md#pick-your-display-mode)). **← Back** closes them.
- **Pop out** moves the Dispatcher out of the menu, into a panel that floats over the map. To put it back, press **Dock back to panel** in the menu. In Multi-Monitor Mode this button is **Workspace** (see [Open the workspace](86-second-monitor.md#open-the-workspace)).
